Privacy Policy

1. 100% Local Processing

ToolBox Pro prioritizes your privacy. Your PDF documents, images, and QR codes are processed locally in your browser's memory. No uploaded files are stored on external application servers.

2. No Personal Data Harvesting

We do not collect personal names, files, passwords, or transaction data. Everything stays on your phone or computer unless you explicitly export or share it.

3. Camera Access

Camera permissions are requested solely for client-side QR code scanning and are never streamed or recorded.
